# REQ-0007 — Frozen, authority-bound task contract

- Status: ACCEPTED
- Priority: P0
- Source: user requirement that AI completion declarations cover the real project task rather than a claimant-selected subset
- Dependencies: REQ-0001, REQ-0002, REQ-0004, REQ-0006

## Statement

Before final acceptance, the task's authority sources, source requirements, scope, blocking criteria, evidence policies, reviewer procedure, exclusions, amendments, and lifecycle shall be frozen in a deterministic Task Contract. Claim and Review artifacts must bind the exact contract and cannot redefine or omit its requirements.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Contract normalization rejects unknown fields, mutable Git refs, unsafe paths, duplicate IDs, unused sources, asymmetric requirement/criterion mappings, and contradictory scope.
2. Contract digest changes when any authority, source, requirement, criterion, named-check policy, review policy, lifecycle, or amendment field changes.
3. Claim binds contract ID/digest, authority declaration, complete criterion snapshot, repository, base, head, and chronology.
4. The final reviewer revalidates one authority receipt for every source through trusted adapters.
5. Review binds the Claim digest, exact repository scope, authority receipt set, reviewer identity, R-level procedure, and chronology.
6. Named-check evidence binds policy, executable, arguments, working directory, result, reviewer, and Claim HEAD.
7. Bare caller-supplied passing strings cannot authorize evidence or lifecycle gates.
8. Claimant-provisional contracts can never yield final PASS.
9. Superseded contracts produce STALE; revoked contracts produce FAIL.
10. Public Author/Reviewer/MCP/Skill paths cannot claim contract-enforced acceptance until TASK-0007 integrates this core.
